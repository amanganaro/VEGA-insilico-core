## Notes

- `FunctionalGroups`: `nRCOX` and `nArCOX` have the same SMARTS (aromatic only), as do `nRCSSH` and `nArCSSH` (aliphatic only): each pair always has identical values. Aliphatic acyl halides (e.g. acetyl chloride) are counted by neither.
- An empty SMILES is converted to a *valid* molecule whose `GetStructure()` fails: every descriptor is MISSING_VALUE (CUR-432).
- `Constitutional` returns MISSING_VALUE for Sv/Mv or Se/Me on elements without van der Waals volume or electronegativity (e.g. Pt, Gd, He, U, Ge, Al).
- `[238U]` loses its isotope in the regenerated SMILES, `[235U]` keeps it; `[C]` becomes methane.
- Fullerene C60 is parsed but rejected by the normalization (kept as invalid on purpose).
